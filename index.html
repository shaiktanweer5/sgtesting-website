ABOUT SG TESTING

Technical expertise for industrial equipment.

SG Testing & Industrial Services supports industrial organizations with equipment inspection, examination and testing.

Our documented procedures cover pressure vessels, hoists, lifts and lifting tackles. Depending on the equipment and approved scope, activities may include visual inspection, dimensional checks, ultrasonic thickness testing, hydrostatic testing, load testing and technical reporting.

We focus on clear observations, appropriate assessment methods and practical communication with our customers.

OUR SERVICES

Industrial inspection & testing solutions.

Our listed core services are supported by SG Testing's supplied standard operating procedures. Final scope is confirmed for each assignment.

01 / INSPECTION
PV

Pressure Vessel Inspection

External and internal examination of pressure vessels, including equipment condition, accessories and thickness assessments where applicable.

02 / TESTING
HT

Hydrostatic Pressure Testing

Hydrostatic testing of pressure vessels or plant with controlled testing procedures, observation of test conditions and recording of results.

03 / INSPECTION
HL

Hoists & Lifts Inspection

Examination of applicable goods lifts, passenger lifts and hydraulic cage lifts, including mechanical components, safety devices and operating condition.

Enquire about service →
04 / LOAD TESTING

Hoists & Lifts Load Testing

Load testing of suitable hoists and lifts using an agreed procedure, with observations and technical records of the test.
