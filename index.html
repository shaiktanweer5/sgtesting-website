WHO WE ARE

Technical services built around industrial needs.

SG Testing & Industrial Services provides industrial equipment inspection and testing supported by documented procedures.

The company's supplied procedures cover pressure vessels, hoists, lifts and lifting tackles. Activities may include visual examination, dimensional measurements, ultrasonic thickness assessment, hydrostatic testing, load testing and technical records, depending on the assignment.
