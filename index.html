WHO WE ARE

Engineering-focused inspection for industrial equipment.

SG Testing & Industrial Services provides inspection and testing services for industrial equipment, with documented procedures for pressure vessels, hoists, lifts and lifting tackles.

Our procedures describe visual examination, dimensional checks, ultrasonic thickness assessment where applicable, hydrostatic testing and load testing, supported by recorded observations and inspection reports.
